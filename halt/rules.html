Overview

The game is played with two armies, one red and one blue (you are red). Each army consists of one king, and several knights (usually eight). The goal is to capture the enemy king with your knights, while avoiding capture yourself.

Movement Rules

  • Players alternate turns.
  • A king or knight may move one space in any direction (including diagonals) as long as the space is unoccupied.
  • A king or knight may push an adjacent knight of any color one space, and move into the space previously occupied. However, it cannot push if the space that the knight would be moved into is occupied. That is, two knights in a row cannot be pushed.
  • You can never push a king.
  • For convenience, legal moves are highlighted in yellow when playing the game.

No Repetition Rule

You may not immediately repeat positions. That is, if one knight pushes another, the pushed knight cannot push back on the next move to repeat the previous position.

Victory Conditions

  • If three knights are adjacent to the enemy king, they capture the king and win the game.
  • If the king is on the side of the board, this goes down to two knights.
  • If the king is in the corner then only one knight is needed.
The latter rules are to prevent the king from cowardly hiding in the corner surrounded by its own knights, where it can be impossible to get three opposing knights adjacent to the king.
